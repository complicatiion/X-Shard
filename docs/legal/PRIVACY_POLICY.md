### 6. Your Statutory Rights under the GDPR
If your personal data is processed (e.g., when you contact the developer via email), you have the following rights under the GDPR:
* **Right of Access (Art. 15 GDPR):** Request information about your processed personal data.
* **Right to Rectification (Art. 16 GDPR):** Request correction of inaccurate data.
* **Right to Erasure (Art. 17 GDPR):** Request deletion of your data, unless legal obligations require retention.
* **Right to Restriction of Processing (Art. 18 GDPR):** Request restricted processing of your data.
* **Right to Data Portability (Art. 20 GDPR) & Right to Object (Art. 21 GDPR):** Object to data processing based on legitimate interests.
* **Right to Lodge a Complaint (Art. 77 GDPR):** You have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent data protection supervisory authority.
